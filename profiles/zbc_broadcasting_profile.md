# Zimbabwe Broadcasting Corporation (ZBC) Authoritative Profile & Broadcasting Architecture

## 1. Overview & Institutional Mandate
The **Zimbabwe Broadcasting Corporation (ZBC)** (`zbc.co.zw` / `zbcnews.co.zw`) is the official state-controlled public service broadcaster of the Republic of Zimbabwe. Operating under the Broadcasting Services Act (Chapter 12:06) and overseen by the Ministry of Information, Publicity and Broadcasting Services, ZBC holds the statutory public mandate to broadcast television and radio programming across Zimbabwe's 16 constitutionally recognized official languages:
- English, Shona, Ndebele, Chewa, Chibarwe, Kalanga, Tsoa, Ndau, Shangani, Sign Language, Sotho, Tonga, Tswana, Venda, Xhosa, and Koisan.

## 2. Headquarters & Facilities
- **Primary Headquarters**: Pockets Hill Broadcasting Complex, Highlands, Harare, Zimbabwe.
- **Regional Studios**: Montrose Studios, Bulawayo (serving the southern regions, Matabeleland North, Matabeleland South, and Midlands).
- **Transmitter Network**: Operated in conjunction with Transmedia Corporation across dozens of terrestrial FM and digital transmission sites nationwide.

## 3. Television Channels
1. **ZBC TV**: Flagship national terrestrial and digital television channel broadcasting news, current affairs, national events, parliamentary sessions, education, and cultural programming.
2. **Jive TV**: Dedicated youth, entertainment, arts, and local musical content channel.

## 4. National Radio Networks
1. **Radio Zimbabwe**: The country's largest national radio station by listenership, broadcasting predominantly in Shona and Ndebele, focusing on rural and urban development, education, and culture.
2. **Power FM**: High-energy contemporary music and urban lifestyle station broadcasting nationwide in English.
3. **Classic 263**: Talk, current affairs, news analysis, jazz, and classic hits broadcasting from Harare.
4. **National FM**: Dedicated minority languages station broadcasting in 14 of Zimbabwe's recognized indigenous languages (Tonga, Venda, Kalanga, Shangani, Sotho, Ndau, etc.).
5. **Khulumani FM**: Bulawayo metropolitan and regional commercial/community radio station broadcasting in Ndebele, English, Kalanga, and Xhosa.
6. **Central Radio**: Regional radio service for the Midlands province broadcasting from Gweru.

## 5. Governance & Digital Transition
- **Regulatory Framework**: Licensed and regulated by the Broadcasting Authority of Zimbabwe (BAZ).
- **Digital Terrestrial Television (DTT)**: Part of Zimbabwe's digital broadcasting migration program modernizing transmission to DVB-T2 standards.
